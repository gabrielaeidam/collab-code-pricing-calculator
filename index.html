Where the suggested rates come from

The suggested rates are The Collab Code's starting points, set at or above these published market averages so you never start out undercharging:

  • UGC video: published averages run about $150 to $212 per video (inBeat, Influee), and Toptal puts established creators at $500+. Suggested here by experience level: just starting out $200, some experience $350, experienced $500, expert $1,000.
  • UGC photo: Influee prices a single product photo at about $80, and Toptal puts a photo set at $100 to $300+. Suggested here by experience level: $50, $65, $75 and $100 per photo.
  • Posted to your page: median paid rates from 1,527 deals, January 2025 to June 2026, were $134 under 10K followers, $150 for 10K to 50K, $417 for 50K to 250K, $517 for 250K to 1M and $2,727 for 1M+ (OpenSponsorship data, reported by inBeat). Suggested here, per video: under 10K $350, 10K to 50K $500, 50K to 75K $750, 75K to 100K $1,000, 100K to 250K $1,500, 250K to 1M $2,500 and 1M+ $5,000, with photos at half the video rate.
  • Views and engagement (optional): posted video rates can be based on average views at $20 per 1,000 views, within IQFluence's 2026 range of about $8 to $25 for Reels from 10K to 100K follower accounts. Engagement is compared to IQFluence's 2026 benchmarks of about 30 million Instagram and 107 million TikTok creators: above the median adds 10%, above the top 25% adds 20%, and above the top 10% adds 30%. Free lookups by Modash.
  • Paid advertising usage, 30% per month: the top of the published range of 20% to 30% of the base rate per month (PitchBrand, Toptal).
  • Ads through your creator account, 30% per month: only offered when you post to your page. Influee and Toptal put whitelisting and Spark Ads at about 30% of the base rate per month.
  • Exclusivity, 15% or 50% of your posted content per month: only offered when you post to your page. A creator coach quoted by Modash charges 10% to 15% of the base rate per month for easier categories and 50% for competitive ones like beauty or fashion.
  • Extras: extra hooks $50 each (Influee, inBeat) and raw footage at 25% of the video rate, below the published 30% to 50% range; rush delivery at 25% of your content, the low end of the 25% to 50% range (Toptal, Influee), with a $50 minimum. Extra versions start at the $50 hook price, and extra revision rounds at $50 each.

These are starting points, not guaranteed earnings. Your rate can and should go up as your work, results and demand grow.
